## EU SME eligibility

**Current decision:** `TO_VERIFY`  
**Group check:** `linked_enterprise_check`

The official imprint also names Bada Hispanaplast S.A. in Spain. An independent
profile describes Bada AG with a Spanish subsidiary and reports 230 employees
and EUR 150 million turnover in 2023 ([source](https://www.liebich-partner.de/kunden/fuehrungskraefteentwicklung-bada-ag/),
`S-P06-02`). A separate industry article reports 115 employees at the Bühl
headquarters ([source](https://www.aktiv-online.de/news/unternehmen-bada-aus-buehl-warum-hier-trotz-krise-investiert-wird-19766),
`S-P06-03`).

The employee count remains below 250, while turnover exceeds EUR 50 million.
Under the EU SME test, eligibility can therefore only be decided after the
aggregated balance-sheet total and all linked/partner enterprises are resolved.
No final score band is assigned before that eligibility gate is closed.

## Process and energy-transition evidence

- Bada develops and produces application-specific compounds from technical
  thermoplastics and thermoplastic elastomers (`S-P06-02`).
- A supplier case study documents an extrusion-line retrofit, including the
  main-drive frequency converter, control-cabinet cooling and process-data
  capture ([source](https://bsg.de/en/erfolgsgeschichten/bada-ag/),
  `S-P06-07`).
- The official certificate index lists ISO 14001, EMAS and ISO 50001
  ([index](https://bada.de/de/zertifikate), `S-P06-04`). The
  [ISO 50001 certificate](https://bada.de/app/uploads/Zertifikat-Bada-50001-2024.pdf)
  is recorded separately as `S-P06-05`.
- The official sustainability page documents recycled compounds, a closed-loop
  approach and BADAGREEN products with at least 25 percent sustainable polymer
  content ([source](https://bada.de/de/nachhaltigkeit), `S-P06-06`).
- Public reporting describes EUR 9 million investment in Bühl and EUR 6 million
  in the Spanish plant, but does not identify those investments as
  decarbonisation investments (`S-P06-03`).

## Provisional scoring interpretation

Scoring remains **open**, not zero. The available evidence supports material
relevance for motor drives, extrusion heating/cooling, controls and power
conversion. It also shows visible transition-management and efficiency
measures, so the transition-gap dimension must not be treated as automatically
high. No quantified site energy consumption, load profile, PV capacity, heat
recovery, storage, process-heat electrification or verified CO2-reduction result
has yet been identified in the reviewed source set.
