## Process evidence

The official website documents injection moulding, multi-component moulding,
inline PVD metallisation, coating, material drying, automated inline production,
robotics, assembly and an internal tool shop (`S-P07-02`). These are
firm-specific signals for motor drives, electric process equipment, vacuum and
coating systems, cooling, power conversion and coordinated controls.

## Existing transition measures

The reviewed sources document:

- more than 50% fully electric injection-moulding machines;
- PV generation reported at more than 600,000 kWh per year;
- centrally controlled, energy-efficient material drying;
- geothermal machine cooling;
- use of process waste heat for building heating;
- heat-pump conversion and stated independence from fossil fuels for building
  heating;
- automated inline processes and resource-saving PVD/coating;
- battery storage and fast-charging infrastructure announced for 2025.

## Interpretation for energy-solution providers

Varioplast is not a classic “hidden decarbonisation gap” lead. It is a more
advanced reference case. The publicly visible opportunity is narrower and more
specific: PV-plus-storage coordination, load management, charging integration,
power quality, peak management and the continued replacement or optimisation of
remaining hydraulic equipment.
